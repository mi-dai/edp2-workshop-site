Registration

Registration for the workshop is now open via Google Forms. There is no registration fee.

Travel support: Thanks to the LSST Discovery Alliance, we are able to offer travel and lodging support for approximately 18 participants, with priority given to early career scientists and those who would otherwise not be able to attend. The registration form will ask you to indicate whether you wish to be considered for support.

Contributed Talks/Tutorials: Day 1 of the workshop will focus on tutorials and context for the science possible with DP2. Please indicate in the registration form if you would like to be considered for giving a short talk or tutorial.

Deadline: To be considered for travel support and/or a contributed talk, please submit your registration before August 21. The registration will close when we reach capacity (~40 participants).
