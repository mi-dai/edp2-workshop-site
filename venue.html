Travel

Airport: The closest airport is Pittsburgh International Airport (PIT), about 20 miles (30–40 minutes) from the University of Pittsburgh campus.

Getting to/from campus:

  • Rideshare/Taxi: Uber and Lyft operate from PIT and are the most convenient option; the ride typically takes 30–40 minutes depending on traffic.
  • Airport Flyer bus (28X): Port Authority of Allegheny County's 28X bus runs between PIT and Downtown/Oakland (near campus) for a low-cost option; check rideprt.org for current schedules and fares.
