Overview

Rubin Observatory's Early Data Preview 2 (EDP2) gives the time-domain community its first real look at processed LSST data. This hands-on workshop brings together time-domain astronomers and cosmologists from DESC, TVS, and the SLSC to work through the analysis challenges posed by EDP2, from transient discovery through spectroscopic follow-up selection, using LINCC Frameworks tools. Along the way, the workshop aims to produce tutorials the community can keep using and to start new collaborations that continue beyond the workshop itself.

Early career scientists and anyone new to Rubin/LSST looking for a way to get involved with EDP2 projects are especially encouraged to join — no prior experience with EDP2 or LINCC Frameworks tools is required.

Funded by the LSST Discovery Alliance through the LINCC Frameworks Community Events program. We gratefully acknowledge their support in making this workshop possible.

Goals

We aim to identify and focus on common, practical time-domain challenges posed by EDP2 and the LSST alert stream: filtering the dataset for transient candidates, classifying and fitting light curves, cross-matching with galaxy catalogs for host-galaxy association and photometry, selecting candidates for spectroscopic follow-up, working through forced- and scene-modeling photometry requirements, folding alert and prompt data products into discovery workflows, and generating realistic simulations to understand selection effects and biases.

The program pairs a handful of invited and contributed talks with dedicated hack days for tutorial-building and hands-on collaboration. We aim to produce public tutorials for the community and begin projects that continue beyond the workshop.
