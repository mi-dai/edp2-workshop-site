Abstract Submission

We welcome abstract submissions for contributed talks related to time-domain science with LSST EDP2, to be featured in the Day 1 contributed talks session. Submission will be handled via Google Forms; details will be posted here once the form is finalized.

Topics of interest include (but are not limited to): transient discovery and filtering, classification and light-curve fitting, host-galaxy association and photometry, spectroscopic follow-up selection, forced and scene-modeling photometry, alert and prompt data product workflows, and simulations for characterizing selection effects.
